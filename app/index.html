    Sources & honesty note

    Grand Theft Auto VI released on 19 November 2026. This guide was written two days later, so most of it is built from the official trophy descriptions, not from playing the game — and it says so wherever that is the case.

    Where the facts come from

    • Official trophy list — names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags as published on PlayStation, via Exophase (exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies) and cross-checked against PSNProfiles.
    • Counts and locations — PushSquare, GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (42 Shooting Range challenges, 397 stunts, 14 drone races, 18 meditation spots, 8 garages and their prices).
    • Cross-checks — Gamemapa (activity types, Criminal Profile actions, the missable meditation report) and KeenGamer (grade table).

    What we do not claim

    • Any guide step marked “not yet checked in-game” is our own advice drawn from the official description. It may be refined as players confirm the details.
    • Hidden flags follow the PlayStation list as published on Exophase (28 hidden of 55). If the live list differs, the flags will be corrected — the names, descriptions and grades are the official ones either way.
    • No Rockstar Games logo, official artwork, box art or game screenshot is used anywhere in this guide. The look is our own.

    Offline & private

    This guide is static files. It makes no network request at run time, stores your progress only in this browser (localStorage), and offers export and import as a file you control.
